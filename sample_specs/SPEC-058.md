# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: ChromaScan
- Model: CC-900
- Equipment Type: Thickness Inspection
- Measurement Principle: Chromatic Confocal
- Inspection Mode: Offline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode
- Maximum Electrode Width: 500 mm

## Measurement Performance

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Measurement Range (Z) | 0 ~ 2000 μm |
| Accuracy | ±6 μm |
| Repeatability | ±3 μm |
| Z Resolution | 5 μm |
| Measurement Speed | 100 mm/s |

## Defect Inspection

- Not Supported

## Optical System

- Sensor: Chromatic Confocal
- Light Source: White LED

## Interface

- PLC: Supported
- MES: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 10 ~ 40 °C
- Humidity: 20 ~ 80 %RH

## Safety

- Emergency Stop: Supported

## Notes

Wide measurement range for thick coating stacks; resolution is coarser than the CC-200/CC-500 line.
