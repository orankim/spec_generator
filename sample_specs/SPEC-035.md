# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: FilmInspect
- Model: FI-500
- Equipment Type: Coating Inspection
- Measurement Principle: Spectral Interferometry
- Inspection Mode: Inline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode Coating
- Maximum Electrode Width: 500 mm

## Measurement Performance

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Measurement Range (Z) | 0.1 ~ 500 μm |
| Accuracy | ±0.8 μm |
| Z Resolution | 0.1 μm |
| Measurement Speed | 600 mm/s |

## Defect Inspection

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Defect Types | Coating Non-uniformity |

## System

- Camera: CMOS
- Light Source: Broadband Light
- Data Output: Ethernet
- PLC Interface: Supported
- MES Interface: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 5 ~ 40 °C
- Humidity: 20 ~ 85 %RH

## Notes

Optimized for inline coating thickness and coating uniformity inspection.
