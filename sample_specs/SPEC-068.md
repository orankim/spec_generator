# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: RangeMax
- Model: RM-600
- Equipment Type: 3D Profile Inspection
- Measurement Principle: 3D Laser Profilometry
- Inspection Mode: Inline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode
- Maximum Electrode Width: 600 mm

## Measurement Performance

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Measurement Range (Z) | 0 ~ 1000 μm |
| Accuracy | ±6 μm |
| Repeatability | ±3 μm |
| Z Resolution | 5 μm |
| Measurement Speed | 300 mm/s |

## Defect Inspection

- Not Supported

## Optical System

- Sensor: Laser
- Light Source: Laser

## Interface

- PLC: Supported
- MES: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 10 ~ 40 °C
- Humidity: 20 ~ 80 %RH

## Safety

- Laser Class: Class 2
- Emergency Stop: Supported

## Notes

Large Z measurement range (0~1000 um) for deep surface features, but Z resolution (5 um) is coarser than the corpus average — range/resolution trade-off.
