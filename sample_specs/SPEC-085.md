# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: DualCheck
- Model: DC-1100
- Equipment Type: Electrode Thickness & Surface Defect Inspection System
- Measurement Principle: Confocal
- Inspection Mode: Offline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode
- Maximum Electrode Width: 400 mm

## Measurement Performance

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Measurement Range (Z) | 0 ~ 100 μm |
| Accuracy | ±0.15 μm |
| Repeatability | ±0.08 μm |
| Z Resolution | 0.03 μm |
| Measurement Speed | 30 mm/s |

## Defect Inspection

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Minimum Detectable Defect | 5 μm |
| Defect Types | Scratch, Pinhole, Coating Non-uniformity, Edge Crack |
| Classification | Supported |

## Optical System

- Sensor: Confocal
- Light Source: White LED

## Interface

- PLC: Supported
- MES: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 10 ~ 40 °C
- Humidity: 20 ~ 80 %RH

## Safety

- Emergency Stop: Supported

## Notes

High-precision offline combined inspection for sample audits; both thickness and fine surface defects are covered.
