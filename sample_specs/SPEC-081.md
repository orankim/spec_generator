# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: DefectOnly
- Model: DF-450
- Equipment Type: Surface Inspection
- Measurement Principle: Line Scan Vision
- Inspection Mode: Inline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode
- Maximum Electrode Width: 1600 mm

## Measurement Performance

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| X Resolution | 25 μm |
| Y Resolution | 25 μm |
| Measurement Accuracy | ±20 μm |
| Line Speed | 600 mm/s |

## Defect Inspection

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Minimum Detectable Defect | 20 μm |
| Defect Types | Scratch, Contamination |
| Classification | Supported |

## Thickness Measurement

- Not Supported

## Optical System

- Camera: CMOS Line Scan
- Light Source: LED

## Interface

- PLC: Supported
- MES: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 10 ~ 40 °C
- Humidity: 20 ~ 80 %RH

## Safety

- Emergency Stop: Supported

## Notes

Wide-width (1600 mm) surface defect inspection; thickness inspection is not supported.
