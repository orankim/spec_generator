# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: DeepOCT
- Model: DO-300
- Equipment Type: Thickness Inspection
- Measurement Principle: OCT
- Inspection Mode: Inline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode
- Maximum Electrode Width: 500 mm

## Measurement Performance

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Measurement Range (Z) | 0 ~ 300 μm |
| Accuracy | ±0.6 μm |
| Repeatability | ±0.3 μm |
| Z Resolution | 0.1 μm |
| Measurement Speed | 350 mm/s |

## Defect Inspection

- Not Supported

## Optical System

- Light Source: SLD
- Wavelength: 840 nm

## Interface

- PLC: Supported
- MES: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 10 ~ 40 °C
- Humidity: 20 ~ 80 %RH

## Safety

- Emergency Stop: Supported

## Notes

Standard inline OCT thickness inspection for battery electrode coating.
