# Equipment Specification

- Manufacturer: NanoDefect
- Model: ND-150
- Equipment Type: Surface Inspection
- Measurement Principle: Confocal Vision
- Inspection Mode: Offline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

- Target: Battery Electrode Surface
- Maximum Electrode Width: 150 mm

- Light Source: White LED
- Data Output: Ethernet
- PLC Interface: Supported
- MES Interface: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 18 ~ 28 °C
- Humidity: 30 ~ 70 %RH

Optimized for offline high-resolution surface defect analysis.
