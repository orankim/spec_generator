# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: CoatingOCT
- Model: CO-400
- Equipment Type: Coating Inspection
- Measurement Principle: OCT
- Inspection Mode: Offline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode Coating
- Maximum Electrode Width: 400 mm

## Measurement Performance

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Measurement Range (Z) | 1 ~ 400 μm |
| Accuracy | ±0.5 μm |
| Z Resolution | 0.1 μm |

## Defect Inspection

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Defect Types | Coating Non-uniformity, Void |

## System

- Camera: CMOS
- Light Source: Near Infrared
- Data Output: Ethernet
- PLC Interface: Supported
- MES Interface: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 18 ~ 28 °C
- Humidity: 30 ~ 70 %RH

## Notes

Designed for offline coating thickness and uniformity characterization.
