# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: DefectOnly
- Model: DO-400
- Equipment Type: Surface Inspection
- Measurement Principle: Vision
- Inspection Mode: Inline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode Surface
- Maximum Electrode Width: 400 mm

## Measurement Performance

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Measurement Speed | 1200 mm/s |

## Defect Inspection

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Minimum Detectable Defect | 8 μm |
| Defect Types | Scratch, Contamination, Particle |

## Thickness Measurement

- Not Supported

## System

- Camera: CMOS
- Light Source: LED
- Data Output: Ethernet
- PLC Interface: Supported
- MES Interface: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 5 ~ 40 °C
- Humidity: 20 ~ 85 %RH

## Notes

Designed for high-speed inline surface defect detection on battery electrodes.
