# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: NIRScan
- Model: NS-1100
- Equipment Type: Thickness Inspection
- Measurement Principle: OCT-NIR
- Inspection Mode: Inline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode
- Maximum Electrode Width: 800 mm

## Measurement Performance

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Measurement Range (Z) | 0 ~ 3000 μm |
| Accuracy | ±3 μm |
| Repeatability | ±1.5 μm |
| Z Resolution | 2 μm |
| Measurement Speed | 500 mm/s |

## Defect Inspection

- Not Supported

## Optical System

- Light Source: SLD (NIR)
- Wavelength: 1310 nm

## Interface

- PLC: Supported
- MES: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 10 ~ 40 °C
- Humidity: 20 ~ 80 %RH

## Safety

- Emergency Stop: Supported

## Notes

Deep measurement range for thick electrode stacks; resolution/accuracy are traded off for range.
