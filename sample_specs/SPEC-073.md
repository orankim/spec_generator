# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: InterferoPrecision
- Model: IP-200
- Equipment Type: Thickness Inspection
- Measurement Principle: White Light Interferometry
- Inspection Mode: Offline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode
- Maximum Electrode Width: 200 mm

## Measurement Performance

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Measurement Range (Z) | 0 ~ 150 μm |
| Accuracy | ±0.09 μm |
| Z Resolution | 0.005 μm |

## Defect Inspection

- Not Supported

## System

- Camera: CMOS
- Light Source: Broadband Light
- Data Output: Ethernet
- PLC Interface: Supported
- MES Interface: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 18 ~ 28 °C
- Humidity: 30 ~ 70 %RH

## Notes

Optimized for offline high-precision electrode thickness measurement.
