# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: RangeMax
- Model: RM-900
- Equipment Type: Thickness Inspection
- Measurement Principle: 3D Laser Profilometry
- Inspection Mode: Offline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode
- Maximum Electrode Width: 500 mm

## Measurement Performance

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Measurement Range (Z) | 0 ~ 1500 μm |
| Accuracy | ±9 μm |
| Repeatability | ±4.5 μm |
| Z Resolution | 8 μm |
| Measurement Speed | 100 mm/s |

## Defect Inspection

- Not Supported

## Optical System

- Sensor: Laser
- Light Source: Laser

## Interface

- PLC: Supported
- MES: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 10 ~ 40 °C
- Humidity: 20 ~ 80 %RH

## Safety

- Laser Class: Class 2
- Emergency Stop: Supported

## Notes

Extended thickness range (0~1500 um) for thick electrode stacks; resolution (8 um) is deliberately relaxed.
