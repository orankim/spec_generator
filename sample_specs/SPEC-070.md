# Equipment Specification

## General

- Manufacturer: OCTNext
- Model: ON-800
- Equipment Type: OCT Inspection
- Measurement Principle: OCT-NIR
- Inspection Mode: Inline
- Measurement Type: Non-contact

## Inspection Target

- Target: Battery Electrode Coating / Internal Structure
- Maximum Electrode Width: 800 mm

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Measurement Range (Z) | 1 ~ 1000 μm |
| Accuracy | ±1.5 μm |
| Z Resolution | 0.5 μm |
| Measurement Speed | 700 mm/s |

## Defect Inspection

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Defect Types | Void, Coating Non-uniformity |

## System

- Camera: CMOS
- Light Source: Near Infrared
- Data Output: Ethernet
- PLC Interface: Supported
- MES Interface: Supported

## Environment

- Operating Temperature: 5 ~ 40 °C
- Humidity: 20 ~ 85 %RH

Combines OCT-based thickness and internal structure inspection for inline use.
